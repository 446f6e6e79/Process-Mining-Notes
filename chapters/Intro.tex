\textit{
These notes are for the course ``Process Mining'' taught by professor Chiara Di Francescomarino. \\\\
They are not official materials; rather, they were created based on the lecture slides solely to help students prepare for the exam. 
The goal of these notes is to provide a simple and clear summary of the main concepts discussed during the course.\\\\
To facilitate exam preparation, we have grouped together several examples and exercises (along with their solutions) taken from the slides. 
Please note that these notes do not cover all topics discussed during the lectures, nor do they go into the same depth as the official material. 
We strongly recommend attending the lectures and studying the official slides in addition to these notes. \\\\
The up-to-date \LaTeX{} source code for this document can be found in the GitHub repository linked on the title page.
You are free to clone the project, modify it, or contribute by opening an issue or submitting a pull request; 
all contributions are greatly appreciated. \\\\
We hope that this document will be helpful in the study of this interesting and important subject. Happy studying! \\\\
The authors.
